Get set up

About 10 minutes, on your phone.

  1. Open the link on your phoneTap Open Trained.best above. If you're on a computer, open trained-best.vercel.app/?join=4FMDPU on your phone instead.
  2. Pick "I have a coach"Isaiah's code, 4FMDPU, fills in by itself so you land on his client list. If it doesn't, type it in.
  3. Answer every question honestlyYour training days, equipment, injuries and the Health Check decide where your program starts. Guessing high gets you a program that's too hard.
  4. Tick both boxes and finishOne for the terms, one for the waiver. If it asks you to confirm your email, tap the link in that email, then log in: your answers are saved.
  5. Wait 2 to 4 minutes for your programIt's being written for you. If it says it didn't finish, tap Try Again on the My Workout tab.
  6. Add it to your home screeniPhone: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: in Chrome tap the menu, then Add to Home screen. Open it from the new icon from now on.
  7. Allow notificationsThat's how you hear when Isaiah messages you or changes your program.

Every week

  • Train on the days you picked. If you miss one, move it in the Week tab instead of skipping it.
  • Log every set as you go: weight and reps, or say it with the mic.
  • Tap Done at the end of each session so it saves and Isaiah sees it.
  • Log any extra training (a class, a run, sparring) under "Did something extra".
  • Message Isaiah in the Coach tab with anything: pain, questions, a session that felt off.

When something's off

In the app: Profile, then Report a problem. Type or say what happened. It opens an email with your phone details filled in; add a screenshot if you can.

Or just tell Isaiah in the Coach tab.

Pain is different: if something hurts in a way that feels wrong, stop that exercise and message Isaiah before your next session.
